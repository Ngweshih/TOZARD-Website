Donate TOZARD

Help fund research, education and community reach-out.

TOZARD's financial resources are used for four main activities: community reach-out, academic donate for resource-poor pupils/students, hands-on scientific research capacity building, and scientific research on zoonoses and parasite-host-vector-environment interactions.

How funds are used

  • Reach-out programmes into targeted communities.
  • Academic donation of resource-poor children and students.
  • Hands-on BSc and MSc scientific research capacity building.
  • Scientific research donating application of the One Health concept in TOZ disease control.
