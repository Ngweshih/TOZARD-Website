Research programme

One Health research across parasites, vectors, hosts and environments.

Emphasis on Trypanosomosis, Onchocerciasis and Zoonoses, with research spanning epidemiology, parasite-host-vector-environment interactions, molecular work, disease surveillance and innovative control strategies.

T

Trypanosomosis

Research described includes tsetse and Tabanids, trypanosome detection, cattle trypanotolerance, vector investigations and molecular identification.

O

Onchocerciasis

Work includes the bovine Onchocerca ochengi model, host-parasite interactions, vaccination and chemotherapy-related studies, and transmission research.

Z

Zoonoses

The programme includes zoonotic disease investigations and research on interactions among parasites, hosts, vectors, environments and ecosystems.

V

Vector-borne diseases

Documented expertise covers ticks, tsetse, mosquitoes, vector bionomics, insecticide resistance and molecular biology.

M

Molecular epidemiology

Research activities include PCR-based detection, molecular identification, pathogen and endosymbiont studies, and genetic markers.

H

One Health

The laboratory seeks to connect biomedical research with community action and related disciplines and social sciences to improve disease-control application.

Research workflow

From field sampling to laboratory evidence and community application.

  1. Investigate disease and vector patterns in field settings.
  2. Collect samples and conduct laboratory analyses.
  3. Generate and interpret empirical evidence.
  4. Combine findings with relevant existing scientific literature.
  5. Transfer useful knowledge through community outreach, workshops and demonstrations.

TOZARD Research Laboratory

A purpose-driven, non-governmental and non-profit organisation based in Bambili, Cameroon, focused on research and community action around Trypanosomosis, Onchocerciasis and Zoonoses.
