Scientific profiles

Core research expertise.

ACHUKWI MBUNKAH DANIEL

Emeritus Director of Research / Professor

Distinguished veterinarian with at least three decades of research in vector-borne diseases in Cameroon. The source describes leadership in DFG projects and national/international grants, including work on molecular epidemiology, host resistance, trypanosomosis, atypical human trypanosomosis, zoonoses and onchocerciasis.

Dr. Yunenui Peter Mahbou

Microbiologist & Biotechnologist

Research spanning food biotechnology and molecular epidemiology of infectious diseases including dengue and chikungunya; PhD in Biotechnology with specialty in virology. The source also describes experience in onchocerciasis projects and Aedes/arbovirus research.

Dr. Antonio Nkondjio Christophe

Senior Researcher, OCEAC

PhD and HDR in parasitology and medical entomology. Expertise includes malaria vectors, arboviruses, human African trypanosomiasis, onchocerciasis, vector bionomics, vector control, insecticide resistance, population genetics and molecular biology.

Dr. Herbert Afegenwi M.

Microbiologist / Senior Lecturer of Virology

PhD in Microbiology and Immunology from the University of Zurich. Research interest in medical virology and viral zoonotic diseases in a One Health context; the source notes teaching, supervision and peer-reviewed publications.

Dr. Ekoti Ajonina Irene

Microbiologist / Molecular Parasitologist

Research, teaching and public-health background with interests in coinfections of parasitic vector-borne diseases and sustainable health solutions using the One Health approach. Expertise includes molecular biology, immunology and microbiology.

Mrs. Wachong Henrietta Fukamchwi

Medical Laboratory Scientist / Public Health

Master of Public Health from the University of Minnesota Minneapolis. The source describes experience in onchocerciasis research projects, laboratory training and community-facing public health work.

Scientific Advisory Board

Technical advice and protocol review.

The SAB is composed of scientifically renowned professors who advise TOZARD and review research protocols. PD. Dr Alfons Renz as the first endorsed member in 2016 and Prof. Kelm Soerge as the second member, elected in 2023.
