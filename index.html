Science that reaches the communities that need it.

What we are

A research and development association grounded in evidence.

TOZARD is based in Bambili, about ten kilometres from Bamenda, Cameroon, with an annex centre of its research laboratory in Ngaoundere.

01

Targeted diseases

Trypanosomosis, Onchocerciasis and Zoonoses (TOZ) are the central disease areas.

02

One Health platform

The organisation seeks an interdisciplinary platform connecting biological science, communities and partner institutions.

03

Evidence to action

Research findings are combined with existing scientific knowledge and disseminated through reach-out programmes in targeted communities.

Why TOZARD exists

Research is designed to meet difficult real-world conditions.

Remote villages where transport, safe water, electricity, communications, health services and educational donations are be limited. TOZARD's model is to take relevant scientific knowledge and practical research capacity into such settings.
