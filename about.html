About TOZARD

Purpose-driven research for disease burden reduction.

TOZARD is non-political, gender sensitive, non-religious, non-governmental and non-profit , with a focus on using available science to empower people suffering from or exposed to targeted diseases.

What we are

TOZARD has a biological science research laboratory used to generate innovative solutions that can be co-opted with existing knowledge and disseminated during reach-out programmes. The organisation also has an annex research laboratory centre in Ngaoundere.

Its scientific approach is evidence-based: knowledge is grounded in accumulated empirical evidence rather than opinion, feeling or intuition.

Strategic aims

Building capacity from the laboratory to the village.

Rural communities

Enable rural communities to reap the benefits of biomedical science research and education, while strengthening their capacity to adopt innovative disease-control strategies.

Students

Donate to intelligent young biological science students from weak socio-economic or resource-poor backgrounds through education and hands-on research opportunities.

Knowledge transfer

Take state-of-the-art knowledge on TOZ diseases to endemic communities through knowledge dissemination systems, training and field demonstrations.

One Health

Use an interdisciplinary innovation platform connecting disease ecology, hosts, vectors, environments and related disciplines and social sciences.

Rationale

Why the work matters in remote settings.

Access barriers

Remote villages where movement may involve long treks and river crossings and where transport, electricity and communication infrastructure can be limited.

Environmental health

Poor hygienic and environmental conditions can cause disease vectors and contribute to a high burden of parasitic infections.

Health-service distance

Some sick people may die before reaching the nearest rudimentary healthcare unit.

Community-led change

TOZARD's response is to combine research, knowledge transfer, training and community leadership around practical disease-control strategies.

TOZARD Research Laboratory

A purpose-driven, non-governmental and non-profit organisation based in Bambili, Cameroon, focused on research and community action around Trypanosomosis, Onchocerciasis and Zoonoses.
