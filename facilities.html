Facilities

A laboratory built for biological research and field-linked investigation.

Fairly well-equipped Research Laboratory in Bambili, an annex centre in Ngaoundere, electronic infrastructure, solar energy and a vehicle asisting field operations.

Bambili-Tubah, Bamenda

Research facility and operational base.

TOZARD operates a research laboratory where concepts and prototype solutions are designed by experts and tested for eventual use in disease-endemic communities. The facility also donates epidemiological surveys and related research activities on TOZ diseases.

Also electronic infrastructure for communication and tracking development trends for community-adopted TOZ disease-control activities.

Field-to-lab continuity.

Field samples collected during investigations are brought to the TOZARD research laboratory and, in documented collaborations, to partner institutions for molecular identification and related analysis.

TOZARD Research Laboratory

A purpose-driven, non-governmental and non-profit organisation based in Bambili, Cameroon, focused on research and community action around Trypanosomosis, Onchocerciasis and Zoonoses.
