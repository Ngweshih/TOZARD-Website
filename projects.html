Projects & grants

Field investigations, laboratory studies and collaborative research.

Past and ongoing projects and grants are presented below.

Field investigation

Molecular detection of Rickettsia africae in ticks from Cameroon

Tick samples were collected from cattle in Ngaoundere, Adamawa. The study investigated rickettsial DNA in Amblyomma variegatum using real-time and conventional PCR assays targeting the ompB gene.

Reported result: 85 of 149 tested A. variegatum ticks were positive for rickettsial DNA.

Trypanosomosis

Tsetse and Tabanid investigations

TOZARD staff and collaborators undertook investigations in remote areas including Gamba and Mboula-Meiganga, using biconical traps. Samples were taken to the TOZARD research laboratory and the University of Bremen for molecular identification of trypanosomes circulating in vectors, cattle and humans.

Onchocerciasis

Bovine Onchocerca ochengi model

Use of the bovine model for population biology studies, chemotherapeutic and vaccination trials targeted at controlling human onchocerciasis.

Ticks & livestock

Cattle ticks in Cameroon

A study collected 19,189 ticks from 60 animals in five herds over March 2012–February 2013 and examined species distribution, infestation and factors affecting tick abundance.

Grant record

Research grants documented for TOZ diseases.

  • DFG German grant: Can onchocerciasis be eliminated? Epidemiological, entomological and parasitological studies in a bovine model in Cameroon — co-investigator.
  • DFG German grant on analysis of host-parasite cross-talk in the bovine model for human onchocerciasis, O. ochengi — co-investigator.
  • CIRAD-EMVT DESI grant to study genetic markers of trypanotolerance in Doayo cattle — principal investigator.
  • Leverhulme Trust Research Grant on tsetse and trypanosomosis pilot studies in Adamawa Province, Cameroon — principal investigator.
  • IFS Competitive Research Grant B/2900-2-2005 on molecular markers of genetic susceptibility to trypanosomosis in Doayo cattle — principal investigator.
  • IFS Competitive Research Grant B/2900-1 on Namchi cattle trypanotolerance under natural trypanosomosis vector challenge — principal investigator.
  • CIRAD-EMVT DESI Montpellier France grant on genetic markers of trypanotolerance in Doayo (Namchi) cattle — principal investigator.
